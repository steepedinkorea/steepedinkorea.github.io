Privacy Policy for Steeped In Korea

Last updated: 2026-08-27

Steeped In Korea ("the app") is a personal-use content publishing tool operated by its creator. This policy explains what data the app accesses when connected to your TikTok account.

What we access: When you authorize the app via TikTok Login Kit, we access basic profile information (user.info.basic) solely to confirm which TikTok account is connected. When you use the Content Posting API integration, the app uploads a video file you created to your own connected TikTok account (video.upload).

What we do not do: We do not download, store, or share any content from your TikTok account. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

Data storage: Authorization tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device and are not transmitted anywhere other than to TikTok's own API.

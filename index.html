Steeped In Korea

Steeped In Korea is a personal content-publishing tool that creates short factual videos about Korean culture, food, language, and travel, then posts them to the creator's own TikTok account (@steepedinkorea). It uses TikTok Login Kit to identify the connected account and the Content Posting API to publish the finished video. No content is downloaded from TikTok or shared with third parties.
